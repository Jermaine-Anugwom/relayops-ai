# Quality bar

The approved build must read as a civic dispatch document at first glance: chronology, evidence, jurisdiction, and next safe action form one decision path. It may not collapse into interchangeable cards or hide the unresolved field.

Reference challenger: [Studio Dumbar Identity](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/studio-dumbar-identity.webp) and its [hero](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/studio-dumbar-identity-hero.webp). The commitment is graphic decisiveness and legible hierarchy, not copied styling.

# RelayOps AI design record

## Shipped direction

Civic Dispatch Sheet is a public-works decision instrument, not an analytics dashboard. A chronological processing rail feeds one dominant evidence sheet; jurisdiction, unresolved evidence, routing rationale, and the next safe operator action remain visible together. Concept seed `19497e5c` established the direction.

## Visual system

- Canvas `#EFE8D8`
- Working surface `#FBF7EB`
- Ink `#15243A`
- Decision signal `#A93410`
- Supporting signal `#1C5673`

The flat civic-ledger material, ruled evidence regions, condensed display type, and restrained signal colors distinguish the interface from a generic card dashboard.

## Interaction and state model

Healthy, uncertain, blocked, and empty fixtures change the status language, decision, evidence, and available action coherently. The rationale control reveals the policy explanation without moving unresolved evidence out of view. No control performs an external action.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

Desktop preserves the chronology, decision sheet, and jurisdiction context in one operating view. Mobile reorders the same information into evidence-first reading order. Status is expressed in text as well as color, controls have visible focus, the page has a skip link, and the primary tap targets meet the 44-pixel minimum.

## Truth boundary

Every visible person, organization, location, record, and outcome is synthetic. The interface is a deterministic visual fixture and does not claim that its displayed records were generated by the API.
